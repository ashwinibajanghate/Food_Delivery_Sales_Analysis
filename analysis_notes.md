Executive Summary
This project analyzes a food delivery dataset to identify the factors driving revenue, order volume, customer behavior, and platform profitability.

Key areas analyzed:
- City Performance
- Cuisine Performance
- Category Performance
- Weekend vs Weekday Trends
- Festival Impact
- Promotion Impact
- Platform Commission Revenue

1. City Analysis    
Business Question   
Which cities contribute the most revenue and orders?    
Findings    
Net Sales
- Mumbai generated the highest Net Sales.
Orders
- Bangalore generated the highest order volume.
Revenue Per Order
- Mumbai generated the highest revenue per order.   
Insight     
Mumbai is a high-value market, while Bangalore is a high-volume market.     
Recommendation  
Focus premium offerings in Mumbai and scale customer acquisition in Bangalore.

2. Cuisine Analysis     
Business Question   
Which cuisines drive business performance?  
Findings    
Net Sales Leader
- Indian cuisine generated the highest Net Sales.   
Orders
- Indian cuisine received the highest order volume.     
Revenue Per Order       
- Chinese cuisine generated the highest revenue per order.  
Insight     
Indian cuisine's success is primarily driven by customer demand and order volume rather than premium pricing.   
Recommendation  
Expand Indian cuisine offerings and investigate successful menu items.

3. Category Analysis    
Business Question   
Which restaurant categories drive revenue?  
Findings    
Revenue Leader
- Fine Dining generated the highest Net Sales.
Revenue Share
- Fine Dining contributed 29.15% of total revenue.  
AOV
- Cafe recorded the highest AOV.    
Insight     
The business depends significantly on Fine Dining revenue.  
Recommendation  
Protect Fine Dining performance while growing other categories to reduce concentration risk.

4. Weekend vs Weekday Analysis  
Findings    
Average Daily Net Sales
- Weekday ≈ 10.66M
- Weekend ≈ 10.59M  
Average Daily Orders
- Nearly identical  
Insight     
Customer demand remains stable throughout the week.     
Recommendation     
Avoid overallocating marketing spend exclusively to weekends.

5. Festival Analysis    
Findings    
Average Daily Net Sales
- Festival Day ≈ 10.87M
- Non-Festival Day ≈ 10.63M     
Average Daily Orders       
- Festival days performed approximately 2–3% better.
Insight     
Festivals positively influence demand but do not create major sales spikes.     
Recommendation  
Continue festival campaigns but do not rely on them as the primary growth driver.

6. Promotion Analysis   
Findings    
Revenue Share   
- Promo Orders ≈ 13% of revenue.    
AOV
- Almost identical between promo and non-promo orders.  
Category Dependence
- All categories show similar promo participation rates (12–13%).   
Insight     
Promotions are distributed uniformly and do not significantly change customer spending behavior.    
Recommendation  
Review promotion strategy and test targeted campaigns instead of broad promotions.

7. Platform Revenue Analysis    
Business Question   
How does the platform generate commission revenue?  
Findings    
Commission Revenue Leader   
Category  	                Commission Share
- Fine Dining	                23.58%
- Delivery Kitchen	            23.52%  
Important Observation   
Fine Dining generated much higher Net Sales, but Delivery Kitchen nearly matched its commission revenue because of its higher commission rate (20% vs 15%).     
Insight     
Revenue leadership and profit leadership are not the same.
While Fine Dining dominates restaurant revenue, Delivery Kitchen contributes nearly equal platform earnings.    
Recommendation  
Delivery Kitchen should remain a strategic growth area because it produces strong platform profitability.

Final Business Recommendations
1. Maintain strong presence in Mumbai to maximize revenue per order.
2. Expand Indian cuisine offerings because it drives the highest demand.
3. Reduce dependence on Fine Dining by growing Delivery Kitchen and Casual Dining.
4. Continue festival campaigns but set realistic expectations.
5. Re-evaluate broad promotion strategies and experiment with targeted promotions.
6. Invest in Delivery Kitchen partnerships due to strong commission profitability.
7. Explore opportunities to increase packaging and commission revenue while maintaining customer satisfaction.
